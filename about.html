About

A whole website about sixty seconds.

Minute is an independent website about reading watches and keeping them accurate.

We explain dials, hands and accuracy in plain language, with tools that let you read a dial and see how a watch drifts over time.

We do not sell watches, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
